Meridis B2b Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 October 2026

This policy explains how personal data is handled when you use Meridis B2b, a business-to-business ordering application.

Contact us to identify the responsible organisation for your account or request.

2. Data processed

  • Account and business details: name, business name, email or username, account identifiers, contact details and delivery or billing addresses supplied by you, your business or your supplier.
  • Authentication information: login credentials submitted to authenticate you and session identifiers used to maintain access.
  • Commercial information: carts, selected products, quantities, order notes, delivery or pickup choices, orders, invoices and payment status made available by the supplier.
  • Device and service information: app version, operating system, network requests and technical information needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.
  • Notification identifiers: if push notifications are enabled, a device registration token and notification permission status.
  • Local preferences: language, remember-me preference, session information and cached cart or product data stored on your device.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information in free-text order notes.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process data to provide account access, display products and invoices, manage orders and delivery preferences, provide support, maintain security and communicate about the service. Processing is based, as applicable, on performance of a contract with you, legitimate interests in providing services to business customers and preventing misuse, and legal obligations concerning commercial records. Optional communications requiring consent are based on that consent. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Device notification permission can be changed in device settings.

4. Recipients and service providers

Relevant information is available to the supplier managing your business account and to authorised staff and service providers supporting hosting, business systems, support and delivery. When enabled, Firebase Cloud Messaging, provided by Google, supports push notifications and processes the device information needed to deliver them. See Firebase privacy information. Data may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to establish or defend legal claims.

5. International transfers

Service providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where this occurs, the responsible organisation must use an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Contact info@nametech.be for details of safeguards applicable to your data.

6. Retention

Account data is retained while the business account is active and as needed to resolve outstanding account matters. Orders and invoices are retained for applicable accounting, tax and legal requirements. Support and security records are retained for the time needed to resolve the issue, investigate misuse or handle claims. Local cache and preferences may remain until you sign out, clear application storage or uninstall the app. Account deletion does not require deletion of records that must be retained by law.

7. Your rights and account deletion

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Email info@nametech.be with the subject “Meridis B2b privacy request” or “Meridis B2b account deletion” and the email associated with your account. Do not send your password. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity and coordinate with your supplier. Where an account deletion option is available in the app, you may also use it.

We normally respond within one month; where legally permitted, a complex request may take up to two additional months, and we will explain the extension. You may complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority or the competent authority where you live or work. See the Belgian authority's complaint information.

8. Security and required information

We use access controls and technical and organisational measures appropriate to the service. No system can guarantee absolute security. Keep your credentials confidential and report suspected unauthorised access. Account authentication and order details are required to provide the corresponding functions; if you do not supply them, those functions may be unavailable.

9. Children and automated decisions

Meridis B2b is intended for business users, not children. The application does not itself make solely automated decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects about you.

10. Updates

We may update this policy to reflect changes to the service or applicable requirements. The effective date will be updated, and material changes will be communicated where required.
